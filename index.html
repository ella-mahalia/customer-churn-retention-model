BUSINESS PROBLEM

Which customers are most likely to leave?

Customer churn directly affects recurring revenue, but retention teams cannot contact every customer with the same level of attention.

This project uses account, contract, service, billing, and demographic information to estimate churn probability and create customer risk groups that can support retention prioritization.

MODEL DEVELOPMENT

From raw customer data to retention risk.

The workflow focuses on reproducible preprocessing, model comparison, validation, and business interpretation.

01

Clean

Converted TotalCharges to numeric values, handled missing records, and separated modeling features from customer identifiers.

02

Transform

Standardized numeric variables and one-hot encoded categorical customer and service attributes.

03

Compare

Evaluated Logistic Regression, Random Forest, and Gradient Boosting with multiple classification metrics.

04

Validate

Used a stratified train/test split and five-fold cross-validation to assess model stability.
